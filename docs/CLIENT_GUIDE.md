# Managing your catalog

A short guide to the admin panel. Sign in at `/admin/login`.

Nothing here needs a developer, and nothing you change can break the shop —
if something looks wrong, change it back and it's fixed.

## How ordering works

There is no checkout and no payment on the website. When a customer taps
**Order on WhatsApp**, it opens WhatsApp on their phone with their list already
written out and sends it to you. You reply as normal, confirm what's in stock,
and tell them the total.

So the website is your shelf; WhatsApp is still your counter.

## Adding a product

**Products → Add a product**

- **Name** — how customers will search for it. Put the brand first: "Vissco
  Knee Cap", not "Knee Cap Vissco".
- **Category** — pick the section it belongs in. Only subcategories can be
  chosen, because that's where products live.
- **Brand** — optional; leave empty for loose or unbranded goods.
- **Selling price** — what the customer pays.
- **MRP** — only fill this in if you sell it for *less* than the printed MRP.
  It shows the higher price struck through with a discount badge. If you sell
  at MRP, leave it empty.
- **Pack size** — "500 ml", "Sizes S, M, L, XL, XXL". For sizes, one entry
  covers the whole range; the customer tells you their size in the chat.
- **Image** — upload one, or paste a link. Photos of the actual product on your
  shelf work well.

## Changing a price

**Products** → find it → **Edit** → change the price → **Save changes**.

It's live immediately. Renaming is also safe — the product's web address stays
the same, so any link you've already shared on WhatsApp keeps working.

## Marking something out of stock

Edit the product and untick **In stock**. It stays visible so customers know you
carry it, but shows "Out of stock" and can't be ordered.

## The homepage

**Homepage** controls the row of products at the top of the site. To add
something to it, edit the product and tick **Show on the homepage**. Use the
arrows on the Homepage screen to reorder them.

**Settings** has a banner line for announcements — offers, holiday hours,
delivery notices. Leave the text empty to hide it.

## Changing your WhatsApp number

**Settings → WhatsApp number for orders.** Every order button on the site
updates straight away. You can type it with or without +91.

## Categories

**Categories** shows your sections. There are two levels — a main section like
*Skin Care*, with subcategories like *Serums* and *Moisturisers* inside it.
Products always go in a subcategory.

You can't delete a category that still has products in it — the site will tell
you how many need moving first. That's deliberate, so products can't vanish by
accident.

## New supplier price lists

Send the PDF to Prince. Bulk price updates and new supplier ranges are loaded in
one go rather than typed in one at a time — send the *whole* updated list, not
just what changed.

Day to day — adding a product, fixing a price, marking something out of stock —
is all yours and takes seconds.
